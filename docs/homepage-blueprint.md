# LoopDesk Homepage Blueprint

Version 1.0

Approved homepage sequence: Hero; Fragmented-commerce problem; Commerce Loop; Platform overview; Customer experience; Merchant operations; Checkout and payments; Commerce growth; Post-purchase and store credit; One platform versus multiple apps; Founder story; Simple pricing; FAQ; Final CTA.

## Section contracts

Each section must use honest, implementation-oriented language, avoid fabricated proof, avoid final pricing amounts, and distinguish planned capabilities from available capabilities.

### 1. Hero
- Communication objective: introduce LoopDesk as a Shopify Commerce Experience Platform.
- Primary audience: Shopify D2C merchants and operators.
- Main message: Commerce without the chaos.
- Supporting message: LoopDesk connects cart, checkout, payments, self-service, operations, and post-purchase experiences.
- Recommended content format: editorial copy, CTA cluster, and structural product preview.
- Visual direction: split layout with abstract storefront, smart-cart, checkout, and workspace panels.
- Primary CTA: Install LoopDesk.
- Secondary CTA: Explore the Platform; optional Book a Demo text link.
- Current capabilities referenced: cart, checkout, payments, customer self-service, merchant operations, post-purchase workflows.
- Planned capabilities referenced: none in the hero.
- Claims guardrails: no metrics, customer counts, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Mobile behavior: text first, stacked CTAs, readable preview.
- Desktop behavior: controlled split composition.
- Future enhancement opportunity: animated product narrative.
